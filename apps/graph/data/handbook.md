# Aurora Industries — Employee handbook (fictional)

## Annual leave
Every employee on a CDI or CDD earns 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year (2.08 days per month worked). Leave is requested in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the direct manager. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; they must be used before March 31. Leave during the plant summer shutdown (first three weeks of August) is imposed for production, maintenance and logistics teams.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager and HR before the start of your shift. A medical certificate is required from the 3rd consecutive day of absence and must be sent within 48 hours. Salary is maintained at 100% for the first 30 days of sick leave after one year of seniority.

## Parental leave
Maternity leave is 16 weeks (6 before birth, 10 after) for the first two children. Paternity / second-parent leave is 25 calendar days, 4 of which are mandatory right after birth. Aurora maintains full salary during maternity and paternity leave. Parental leave (congé parental) can be taken after one year of seniority; talk to your HR business partner to plan it.

## Remote work charter
Remote work is available for eligible office roles (Commercial, R&D, Finance, IT, Methods, Supply, Quality assurance) up to 2 days per week, with manager approval. Production, maintenance and warehouse roles are not eligible but can request shift changes instead. Working from abroad is allowed up to 20 days per year after HR validation (tax and insurance check) using the remote-abroad form.

## Working hours, shifts and overtime
The standard week is 35 hours. Production runs in 3 shifts (5h–13h, 13h–21h, 21h–5h). Night shifts carry a 25% bonus. Overtime must be approved by the manager in advance; it is paid at 125% for the first 8 hours per week and 150% beyond, or recovered as time off within 3 months. Saturday shifts are limited to 2 per month per person.

## Payroll
Salaries are paid on the last working day of the month. Payslips are available in the HR portal. Changes received before the 20th (bank details, overtime, bonuses) apply to the same month. HR never asks for bank details by email: always use the portal.

## Benefits
The company mutuelle (health insurance) covers 80% of dental and optical costs; dependants can be added with the dependant form. Meal vouchers worth 10 EUR per worked day are provided (60% paid by Aurora). The works council (CSE) offers holiday vouchers and a culture/sports budget. A yearly profit-sharing bonus (intéressement) is paid in May.

## Training
Every employee can request training through their manager or HR; requests are collected each autumn for the next year's training plan. Mandatory safety trainings (CACES, electrical clearance, first aid) are renewed on schedule and tracked in Astro. Certification costs are covered when they relate to the job.

## Career and internal mobility
Internal openings are published on the internal job board first. Internal applications are confidential until the interview stage. Transfers are possible after 12 months in the current role. Annual reviews take place in January–February; salary reviews follow in March.

## Onboarding and trial period
New joiners get a 30-day and a 90-day check-in with HR, a buddy, and an onboarding journey in Astro. The trial period is 2 months for workers and employees, 3 months for technicians and supervisors, 4 months for managers (renewable once).

## Expenses and travel
Expense reports are submitted before the 25th of the month with receipts. Mileage is reimbursed using the official scale. Train travel is the default for trips under 4 hours.

## Equipment and PPE
Personal protective equipment (safety shoes, glasses, gloves, hearing protection) is free and replaced on request at the store. IT equipment issues go through the IT ticketing tool.

## Wellbeing and conflicts
A free and confidential employee assistance programme is available 24/7 (0 800 000 000, fictional). Any conflict or inappropriate behaviour can be reported to HR or the works council; reports are handled confidentially and without retaliation.

## Resignation and offboarding
The notice period is 1 month for workers and employees, 2 months for technicians and supervisors, 3 months for managers. HR starts an offboarding journey in Astro (handover, equipment return, account deactivation).
